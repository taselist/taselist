How to Make the Card Holder

Materials

Cover the backside of the main template with glue.

Fold gently so the corners meet, then crease the fold carefully.

Cut out the holder slots.

Add some tape and fold it over the top.

Fold tape around edge of the main template.

Fold in the flaps to meet the center line at it's respective edges. Crease the folded edges.

Fold tape over the creased edges so it looks like this.

Cover the inner left flap with glue, then press down into place.

Unfold the right flap and arrange the slots with the skinny one at the bottom.

Put glue on the backside slot flaps and press into place, one at a time.

Fold the large right flap back into place and fold the holder slots across.

Flip it over. Apply glue to the two small flaps and press them into place.
Then apply glue to the large flaps, fold them up and press them into place.
Don't make it too tight so you can fit a good amount of cards.

Flip it over and it should look like this.

Now you can apply reinforcement tape to the inside and outside cover.
Go all the way around with each piece of tape. Layer the tape until you reach the edge.

Apply tape across the lower slot and around to the spine like you see in the third picture above.

Do the same thing on the top slot.

Now you can reinforce the upper and lower edges of the slots.
Starting at the lower slot, go around the outside of the back cover and come back around the top.
Layer it until you reach the edge.

Now you can reinforce the spine and close the cover.

Now you can fill up the front holder slot with cards. When you want to work out you can flip your current day's card to enter work out mode. Then you can put it back in the window. When you're finished with a card, you can slot it in behind the holder window. Push yourself and listen to your body. Set goals and complete them. Praise Be.
